A claim decided by the terms, not by whoever opens the inbox.

The insurer writes the policy text, the cap and the deductible on chain. The holder files a claim with an evidence page. One consensus round names the clause it relied on and answers covered, partial or excluded, then the contract does the arithmetic and writes a settlement.

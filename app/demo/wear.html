Service note — hinge and chassis wear

Northside Repair · service 8902 · 18 March · same device (serial …7K92)

What the customer asked

The customer asked for a second inspection of the same device, two weeks after the screen was replaced, now describing "a wobbly lid and scuffed corners".

What we found

  • The hinge is loose after 4 years of use. The service interval in the manual is 24 months.
  • Both rear corners show paint wear down to the magnesium. There is no deformation of the chassis.
  • The display panel replaced on 12 March is intact and correctly seated. No new impact damage.
  • There is no functional fault: the lid holds position, the panel is secure, the device boots.

Technician note

"Everything in this visit is cosmetic and expected wear for a four-year-old machine. Nothing here is impact damage, and nothing here affects how the device works. Replacing the hinge is maintenance, not a repair of damage."

Quoted as maintenance, not damage
